Men's Sessions

Chaperone policy

For every men's session — hijama, cupping, or massage, whether at the weekly clinic day or a mobile visit — a female chaperone is present in the room for the whole appointment. This is a fixed part of how haloe operates, out of respect and for everyone's comfort and safety.

Who can be the chaperone

Any woman the client brings along and trusts to be present — most often a wife, sister, or mother, but any other woman is fine too. She doesn't need to book or pay for anything herself; she's simply there for the session.

How it works

  • The chaperone stays in the room for the entire session, not just at the start.
  • She's welcome to sit, read, or just be present — there's no expectation beyond being there.
  • This applies equally at the Tuesday clinic day and on mobile visits.

If a chaperone can't attend

The session can't go ahead without a chaperone present, so if one isn't able to attend on the day, the appointment will be rescheduled rather than carried out. Where this falls inside the standard cancellation window, the usual cancellation terms apply: free reschedule or full refund up to 48 hours before the session; inside 48 hours, non-refundable but can be moved once; no-shows are charged in full.

Questions about this policy? Message Halima on WhatsApp or via halima@haloe.health — she's happy to talk it through before you book.

FAQ

Swipe through the usual questions.

1 of 8

How much does it cost?

Prices are shown on each session above and in full on the booking page, with the 20% introductory price already applied. Home visits add travel from £20, shown before you pay; the Tuesday clinic on Deansgate has no travel fee.

2 of 8

Do you treat men and women?

Yes. haloe serves women and men, with the same private room, the same standards and the same care for modesty in every session.

3 of 8

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothes you can move in. For back and shoulder sessions a top that opens or lifts easily helps. Only the area being treated is uncovered, and only for as long as it needs to be.

4 of 8

What should I do before and after?

Eat lightly beforehand and stay hydrated. Afterwards, rest, keep drinking water, and keep the area clean, dry and warm for 24 hours. Marks are normal and usually fade within 14 days.

5 of 8

Are you insured and accredited?

Yes. Halima is fully insured and holds a Level 5 Diploma in Health and Cupping Therapy from The Suuk Academy (88% distinction), a CMA and IPHM accredited course.

6 of 8

Where is the clinic, and do you do home visits?

Every Tuesday at Milton Hall, 244 Deansgate, Manchester M3 4BQ, in a private treatment room. On other days (Monday to Saturday, 10am to 8pm) Halima comes to you at home across Greater Manchester. Coaching is also available online.

7 of 8

Is any area not treated?

For modesty and professional care, I do not treat the area between the navel and the knees on male clients. That area stays covered throughout the session. Everything else is treated as booked.

8 of 8

What if I need to cancel or move my session?

Free reschedule or full refund up to 48 hours before your session. Inside 48 hours, sessions are non-refundable but can be moved once. No-shows are charged in full.

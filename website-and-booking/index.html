About haloe

Healing rooted in the Sunnah

Hijama — wet cupping — is one of the oldest traditions in natural wellbeing, practised for thousands of years and encouraged by the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. It uses gentle suction and a few small, superficial incisions; most clients describe feeling lighter and more settled afterwards.

haloe offers three kinds of session — massage to ease everyday tension, dry cupping (suction only, skin unbroken), and wet cupping — hijama itself.

Run by Halima Yasmin, a CMA and IPHM accredited therapist who graduated with an 88% distinction from The Suuk Academy's L5 Diploma in Health and Cupping Therapy.

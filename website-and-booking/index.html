About haloe

Healing rooted in the Sunnah

Hijama — wet cupping — is one of the oldest traditions in natural wellbeing. Practised for thousands of years and encouraged by the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, it uses gentle suction and a few small, superficial incisions. Most clients describe feeling lighter and more settled afterwards.

haloe offers three kinds of session. Massage to ease everyday tension and help you unwind. Dry cupping, which uses suction alone and leaves the skin unbroken. And wet cupping — hijama itself. Each can be booked on its own, or as a package of sessions spread over several weeks.

It is a practice for women and men based in Manchester, run by Halima Yasmin — a CMA and IPHM accredited therapist who graduated with an 88% distinction from The Suuk Academy's L5 Diploma in Health and Cupping Therapy.

Every session is private, dignified, and designed entirely around your comfort — I come to you, so you can rest in your own space afterwards. Flat pricing, unlimited cups, and take-home black seed oil aftercare every time.

Weekly Clinic Day

Tuesdays at Milton Hall

Every Tuesday, Halima holds a full day of appointments at Milton Hall on Deansgate — a private treatment room, not a shared space. A receptionist greets you and the room itself is behind a key-coded door, so your session stays just as private as a home visit.

The building has lift access throughout. Sessions are paid in full at the time of booking, and there's no address to arrange or travel fee to work out — just turn up at your appointment time.

Your journey

What to expect

  1. 1

    Book and tell us about you You are here

    Pick a time online, at the clinic or at home. A short health form comes before your session so nothing is a surprise on the day.

  2. 2

    Your session

    Rest, drink water and keep the area clean and dry for 24 hours. Marks are normal and usually fade within two weeks.

    Your rhythm

    Many clients come back on a rhythm that suits them. We'll shape it around what you came in with.

About Halima Yasmin

Halima Yasmin is a hijama, dry cupping and massage practitioner in Manchester. She founded haloe to give women and men a private, careful place to be looked after.

At A Glance

The short version

Who: Halima Yasmin, founder of haloe.

What she does: hijama (wet cupping), dry cupping, massage and life coaching.

Who she works with: women and men.

Training: Level 5 Diploma in Health and Cupping Therapy, The Suuk Academy, awarded with distinction (88%). The course is accredited by the CMA and IPHM.

Insurance: fully insured.

Where: Tuesdays at Milton Hall, 244 Deansgate, Manchester M3 4BQ, in a private room. Home visits across Greater Manchester on other days.

Her Practice

Why haloe exists

Hijama is a Sunnah of the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. Halima practises it to modern professional standards: a private room, a health form before you begin, clear aftercare and an honest conversation before anything starts.

haloe is for women and men. Many clients come for the shoulders and neck, for a back that has had enough, or for support around their monthly cycle. Others come after training, or because they want one quiet hour that is only theirs. You do not need to know which treatment you want. Halima will talk it through with you.

Qualifications

How Halima is trained

Halima holds a Level 5 Diploma in Health and Cupping Therapy from The Suuk Academy, awarded with distinction (88%). The course is accredited by the CMA and IPHM, two bodies that accredit complementary therapy training. Halima trained on that accredited course.

She is fully insured, and she works within the limits of her training. haloe offers complementary wellness care for relaxation and everyday tension. It is not medical treatment, and Halima does not diagnose or claim to treat any condition. If something needs a doctor, she will tell you so.

How She Works

What a session with Halima is like

You start with a conversation. Halima asks how you are, where you hold tension and what you would like from the session, and she asks you to complete a short health form so she knows what is safe for you.

The room is private and quiet. Only the area being worked on is uncovered, and only for as long as it needs to be. For women and men alike, modesty is looked after as part of every session. You can ask her to stop or change anything at any time.

Afterwards you get clear aftercare in plain words, including what is normal to feel and when to message her. She answers on WhatsApp herself.

Prices are set out on each treatment page and on the booking page, and you pay the full price when you book. Home visits have a travel fee based on distance, shown before you pay. The Tuesday clinic has no travel fee.

What She Offers

Treatments

Hijama (wet cupping), dry cupping, massage and life coaching. They can be booked on their own or combined in one visit.

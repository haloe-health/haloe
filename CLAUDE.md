# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## What this is

Marketing site + booking flow for **haloe**, a hijama/cupping & massage wellness business in Manchester serving women and men. (The booking flow no longer collects gender or surfaces a chaperone requirement — Halima now handles that in person with clients; `chaperone-policy.html` was removed in Sep 2026, don't reintroduce a link to it.) Everything lives under `website-and-booking/`. There is no build step, framework, package manager, or test suite — the site is hand-written static HTML with inline `<style>` and inline `<script>`, plus a handful of Cloudflare Pages Functions and a Supabase (Postgres) database. (D1 was used for booking slot reservation until Sep 2026 — see the migration note under Slot reservation below. It's no longer used anywhere in this codebase.)

**Service model: Weekly Clinic Day: Tuesdays at Milton Hall, 244 Deansgate (Remedy Lounge), full day. Mobile visits across Greater Manchester on other days.** (see `CLINIC_VENUE_NAME`/`CLINIC_WEEKDAY`/etc. constants in `book.html`, `index.html` and the Functions — `CLINIC_START_DATE` is `'2026-09-29'`, the first bookable Tuesday, confirmed by Halima after her venue viewing; before this constant was set, the calendar showed no bookable clinic dates at all rather than defaulting to the next Tuesday, so if it's ever unset again expect that same fail-closed behaviour, not an error). The booking flow has a **Where step** (clinic / mobile) before date & time. **All bookings — clinic and mobile — are paid in full at checkout. There is no deposit option; do not reintroduce a deposit path.** Clinic bookings have no travel fee and no client address (just the venue address and a check-in note); mobile bookings keep the existing calendar, address fields and travel note. Do not remove the Where step, and do not add any further clinic locations without confirming with Halima first.

**Cancellation policy (all bookings):** "Free reschedule or full refund up to 48 hours before your session. Inside 48 hours, sessions are non-refundable but can be moved once. No-shows are charged in full." This replaced the old £25-deposit cancellation wording — keep it identical everywhere it appears (`book.html`, `index.html`, `booking-confirmed.html`, `before-your-session.html`, the Stripe checkout description, and the confirmation emails).

**Travel cost:** free within Manchester city centre; outside it, charged at the actual cost of a taxi from the centre (not a flat fee, not banded). Confirmed by Halima after booking. This wording appears in `book.html` (step 1), `booking-confirmed.html` (step 2) and `before-your-session.html` — keep all three identical.

## Architecture

### Pages
- `index.html` — the full marketing site (hero, conditions, Clinic Day section, "what to expect", footer) **plus a self-contained booking widget** (`HB_SERVICES`, `hb*` functions) in a `#book` section that is currently `display:none` (not shown to visitors — leave it hidden unless asked to re-enable it). The widget does **not** take payment — it collects details and opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message. Its treatment list must be kept identical to `SERVICES` (see `services-data.js` below). The widget has its own Where step (`hbState.loc`) and clinic calendar/slot logic mirroring `book.html`'s.
  - **The conditions hexagons open a modal**, and each entry in that modal's `data` object carries a `cta: { cat, svc }` that becomes a deep link into `book.html` (see below). Every `svc` must match a `SERVICES` name **exactly** — a typo silently drops the preselection and the client lands on a category with nothing chosen. Skin & Beauty maps to a single dry-cupping treatment (there is no package for it); Spiritual Wellbeing intentionally opens the wet-cupping list with nothing preselected.
  - **`#clinic-day`** is the homepage's Clinic Day marketing section (venue, private-room/receptionist/lift/full-payment facts, address, a "Book a Tuesday slot" CTA to `book.html?location=clinic`), linked from both the nav and footer. It's always visible, independent of whether `CLINIC_START_DATE` is set.
- `book.html` — the 5-step booking wizard (Where → Treatment → Date & Time → Details → Confirm) and **the only path that charges money**. State lives in one in-memory `state` object; panels toggle via `.panel.active` and `goToStep(n)`. `SERVICES` and `netPrice()` are loaded from `services-data.js` (see Conventions), which is also the source `hijama-manchester.html`'s price list reads from. Every treatment carries a `desc` (one compliant wellness line — see Compliance); packages carry theirs too.
  - **Layout is a single centred 560px column** (`.book-shell`) with a **frosted footer** (`.footbar`) carrying the running selection and total. `updateRail()` populates `#foot-sum` / `#foot-total` and calls `syncFooter()`, which sets the footer button's label and disabled state and hides the whole bar on step 5 (Confirm). It runs on every state change — `selectLocation`, `selectService`, the category-tab handler, `selectDate`, `selectTime`, `goToStep` and `checkStep3`.
  - **Progress is dots**, not numbered steps: the five `.step` elements are restyled into dots (the active one a gold pill). One adaptive back control, `#back-control`, reads "Back to site" on step 1 and "Back" thereafter. There is **no** centered `<header>` logo (the original big logo SVG is kept in a `display:none` div).
  - **Step 2's category bar (`.category-tabs`) is a full-width four-column grid**, not the base `.segmented` inline-flex — it stretches edge to edge with rounder corners (16px track, 13px segments). Each treatment renders via `renderServices()` as a row with a **gold duration chip** (`.svc-chip`) on its own line and the `desc` beneath, price on the right (the "D4" layout). A selected row is shown **only by its ring filling gold** — there is deliberately no background tint or checkmark, both of which read as a boxed cell against the neighbouring hairlines.
  - **Step 3 puts the calendar and time slots side by side** via `.dt-cols` (grid, collapses to one column ≤720px). `#time-section` is always in the DOM; before a date is picked it shows `#time-hint`, which `selectDate` hides.
  - **It accepts deep links:** `book.html?cat=<category>&svc=<exact treatment name>` preselects a treatment (`applyDeepLink()`, matched by **exact name** — an unknown `cat`/`svc` degrades to the normal Massage default rather than erroring), and `book.html?location=clinic|mobile` pre-selects the Where step via `selectLocation()`. The two combine for the homepage's Clinic Day CTA and the conditions-modal deep links.
  - **Step 5 shows the booking as an "appointment pass"** — date and time as the hero, a perforated edge, then treatment and price. `renderSummary()` builds it. The single pay button carries the real amount via `fullLabel()` — **every booking is paid in full, there is no deposit path** (see Service model above).
- `hijama-manchester.html` — SEO/content landing page (what hijama is, Sunnah days, women & men, a data-driven price list, the Clinic Day block, cancellation policy, a 6-question FAQ with `FAQPage` JSON-LD). Its price list reads `SERVICES`/`netPrice()` from `services-data.js` — **never hardcode prices here**, they'll drift from `book.html`. Linked from `index.html`'s footer.
- `booking-confirmed.html` — Stripe's `success_url` landing page. Reads the booking from the query string and shows "You're booked." plus **three** numbered next steps (Halima will be in touch → on the day, location-aware → cancellation policy), with "Back to haloe" as the only action. The **WhatsApp hand-off has been removed** — the webhook already notifies Halima automatically, so the button was redundant. It also clears `haloe_address` from `sessionStorage`, since nothing needs it once the WhatsApp message is gone.
- `intake.html` — the on-site 5-step health intake form, POSTing to `/intake-submit`. **This replaced the old Google Form.** Do not reintroduce `forms.gle` links. Re-verified end to end on 5 Aug 2026 after the Supabase migration — a real submission wrote a full row to `intake_forms` with every field mapped (text, Yes/No, booleans, optional-nulls). Its text is sized to match `book.html` (labels/values ~1.05rem); long question labels **stack above full-width inputs** (the label-left/value-right row only suits short labels like the booking form's), and questions with **3+ options list vertically** (`:has(.opt:nth-of-type(3))`) so long answers (diabetes, blood-borne) don't clip. Consent toggles are iOS switches — a legacy checkbox-tick `::after` had to be overridden in the checked state or the knob clipped to a sliver.
  - **The 41-key payload in `collect()` is a hard contract.** Text fields and checkboxes are read by **`id`**, radio groups by **`name`**. Rename either and that answer is silently dropped — the form still reports success. Wrapper classes, label text and CSS are all safe to change; **`id` and `name` are not.** After any edit, re-run the check: every id `collect()` reads must still exist in the DOM (41 keys, 41 ids).
  - **Step 2 uses progressive disclosure.** The three "please list them" boxes carry `.field.reveal` and stay hidden until their question is answered Yes. `main_concern` and `safety_notes` follow a question row too but are **always** visible — never hide follow-ups with a blanket sibling rule. See `COMPONENTS.md`.
  - **Question runs are welded by a `.group` wrapper, not `:has()` adjacency.** The old adjacency chain broke the moment anything (like a hidden `.reveal`) sat between two rows, turning each question into its own island. Prefer the wrapper.
- `before-your-session.html` — pre-session guide, linked from the intake confirmation email.

### Functions (`functions/` maps to routes)
- `create-checkout.js` → `/create-checkout`. Creates a Stripe Checkout Session via the REST API (no SDK), embedding booking fields in `metadata[...]`.
- `stripe-webhook.js` → `/stripe-webhook`. Verifies the Stripe signature manually with Web Crypto (HMAC-SHA256, 5-minute timestamp tolerance), then on `checkout.session.completed` sends: a customer confirmation email, Halima's notification email (both via Resend), and a WhatsApp alert to Halima (via Twilio). The customer email **no longer links the intake form** — see the intake note under Conventions. Both emails share a **date/time-hero + grouped-card layout** built from `_email.js`'s `heroRow`/`infoCard` helpers, render in **Poppins with an Arial fallback**, and sit on a full-bleed black background. Halima's notification shows Location as **"Clinic Day"** or **"Home visit"** (derived from `metadata[location]`, with `metadata[venue]` for clinic bookings) and postcodes are uppercased via `upperPostcode`. Both functions should pass `node --check` after editing; an unbalanced template literal in the email HTML kills confirmations silently.
- `intake-submit.js` → `/intake-submit`. Upserts the client and writes an `intake_forms` row to **Supabase (Postgres)** via its REST API (PostgREST) using the **service-role key** — which bypasses RLS, so the tables stay locked to the public anon key while this Function keeps full write access. Then emails the pre-session guide. POST only — intake data is never publicly readable. **Migrated from Cloudflare D1 (`haloe-clients`, `DB` binding) in Aug 2026;** the schema lives in `supabase-schema.sql` at the repo root (two tables, `clients` + `intake_forms`, matching the old D1 model). Needs env vars `SUPABASE_URL` + `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`.
- `availability.js` → `/availability?date=YYYY-MM-DD`. Read-only; returns `{ busy: [{s,e}] }` (start/end minutes) for active bookings on that date. Fails open (returns empty) on any error so a lookup fault never blocks booking.
- `_email.js` — shared brand tokens and email helpers. Exports a `FONT` constant (Poppins + Arial fallback), `heroRow(eyebrow, big, sub)` (centred date/time hero card) and `infoCard(rows)` (grouped inset label/value rows, long values wrap) — both booking emails are built from these; `emailShell` wraps everything and puts the black background on a **full-width table cell** (bgcolor) so it renders full-bleed in every client (a `<div>` background left a gap in Samsung Mail). `_bookings.js` — slot-reservation helpers (time/duration parsing, atomic reserve, confirm/release, `listBookings` for the admin page) — talks to Supabase, not D1, see below. `_supabase.js` — the shared `sbRequest(env, {path, method, prefer, body})` helper both `_bookings.js` and `intake-submit.js` call; one place for the PostgREST fetch/header boilerplate. The `_` prefix keeps all three from becoming routes.
- `admin/_middleware.js` → gates **every** route under `/admin` (Basic Auth against `ADMIN_PASSWORD`, any username accepted). `admin/index.js` → `/admin`, the booking calendar (inline HTML/CSS/JS, same hand-written-per-page convention as the rest of the site — it's just served from a Function instead of a static file so the middleware can sit in front of it). `admin/bookings.js` → `/admin/bookings`, read-only JSON for the page (confirmed bookings + not-yet-lapsed pending holds). Added Sep 2026 — see the admin section below.

### Slot reservation / double-booking prevention (Supabase `bookings` table)
- **Migrated from Cloudflare D1 to Supabase (Postgres) in Sep 2026** — no data carried over; the only D1 rows were Halima's own £1 live-mode tests, deleted rather than migrated. The schema lives in `supabase-bookings-schema.sql` at the repo root: `id`, `booking_date` (native `date`), `start_min`/`end_min` (minutes from midnight), `treatment`, `customer_name`/`email`/`phone`, `location` (`'clinic'|'mobile'`), `address` (clinic venue string or the client's mobile address), `amount_pence`, status `'pending'|'confirmed'`, `hold_expires_at` (unix seconds — deliberately kept as bigint, not `timestamptz`, so the existing `HOLD_SECONDS`/`Date.now()/1000` arithmetic didn't need to change). No `stripe_session_id` column this time — the D1 one was dead (defined, never populated or read).
- **D1/SQLite serializes all writes, so a plain "check-then-insert" was atomic there for free. Postgres has real concurrent writers, so it is NOT safe to run the overlap check and the insert as two separate REST calls** — two simultaneous requests could both pass the check before either commits. `reserveSlot()` in `_bookings.js` instead calls a Postgres function, `reserve_slot` (defined in the schema file, invoked via Supabase's RPC endpoint `POST /rest/v1/rpc/reserve_slot`), which takes a transaction-scoped advisory lock keyed by `booking_date` before checking for overlap — every reservation attempt for the same day is serialized, replicating D1's guarantee without serializing different days against each other. **If you ever touch `reserveSlot()`, keep the check-and-insert inside that one Postgres function — do not "simplify" it back into two REST calls from the Worker, that reopens the race.**
- `create-checkout.js` reserves the slot **before** creating the Stripe session. A win writes a `'pending'` row held for `HOLD_SECONDS` (35 min) and passes `metadata[bookingId]` + `expires_at` to Stripe. A loss (the RPC returns `null`) returns **409 `slot_taken`**, and `book.html` sends the customer back to pick another time. Stripe failure releases the hold.
- `stripe-webhook.js` flips the row to `'confirmed'` on `checkout.session.completed`. Abandoned checkouts never confirm; their holds lapse and the overlap check ignores expired pending rows, so the slot frees itself.
- **Fail-open everywhere:** if `SUPABASE_URL`/`SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` aren't set or the time can't be parsed, checkout proceeds unreserved rather than blocking a paying customer.
- **Known limitations:** the homepage widget (`index.html`) takes no payment, so it neither reserves nor reflects availability — those WhatsApp bookings aren't in the database and need Halima's manual eye. 8pm stays bookable for any treatment (no "runs past closing" rule).

### Admin calendar (`/admin`)
- Password-gated via `functions/admin/_middleware.js` (HTTP Basic Auth, checked against the `ADMIN_PASSWORD` env var — any username works, only the password matters). The middleware covers the whole `/admin/*` prefix, so both the page and its `/admin/bookings` JSON API require the password; there's no way to reach either without it.
- `admin/index.js` renders the calendar: bookings grouped by date, with Upcoming/Past and Clinic Day/Mobile filters (client-side, instant, re-filters the one fetched list rather than re-querying). Each row has `tel:`/`wa.me` links (phone normalized to `44…` for WhatsApp) and shows treatment, location + address, amount paid, and status (`confirmed` → "Paid", `pending` → "Pending payment").
- **Conflict flagging is a client-side overlap check over whatever `listBookings()` returns** — it doesn't trust the reservation system to have caught everything, since the homepage widget's WhatsApp bookings bypass the database entirely (see the known limitation above) and could collide with a `book.html` booking without either side knowing.
- **"Copy today's clinic list"** builds `HH:MM am/pm — Name` lines (name + time only, deliberately no other detail) for confirmed clinic bookings dated today, and copies via `navigator.clipboard.writeText`.
- Set `ADMIN_PASSWORD` in the Cloudflare Pages dashboard like the other secrets below. For local testing, add it to `.dev.vars` (gitignored) and don't commit it.

### Booking → payment → notification flow (the critical path)
1. `book.html` collects selection + details into `state`, then `handlePayment()` POSTs to `/create-checkout`. Every booking is paid in full — there is no deposit type or amount to choose.
2. The function creates the Checkout Session and sets `success_url` to `booking-confirmed.html` with booking details as query params.
3. **Notification is automatic and does not depend on the customer.** Stripe calls `/stripe-webhook`, which emails Halima and the customer (and would WhatsApp Halima if Twilio were configured — it deliberately isn't). Nothing on the confirmation page is required for Halima to find out about a booking.
4. The WhatsApp alert comes from the Twilio business number, **not** the client's number — sending as the client is impossible and would be impersonation. The client's number is in the message body so Halima can reply.

### Design language (all customer-facing pages)
Reusable patterns are documented in **`COMPONENTS.md`** (repo root) — copy-paste snippets, since the site keeps CSS/JS inline per page. Update that file first when you improve one.

`book.html`, `booking-confirmed.html` and `intake.html` share one Apple-derived language — **true black, neutral greys (`#1C1C1E` surfaces, `rgba(255,255,255,0.10)` hairlines), gold used only for the active selection**, sentence-case headings with full stops, and `html { font-size: 78% }` as the single scale dial. Patterns to reuse rather than reinvent:
- **Grouped inset lists** (iOS Settings): label left, answer right, hairline between rows, 12px radius. Build them with a **`.group` wrapper that owns the card**, not per-row `:has()` adjacency rules.
- **Segmented controls** for either/or answers; **toggle switches** for consents. Use a segmented control for anything that reads as a *question* — a lone switch among questions looks out of place. Options must have **no border**; `intake.html` carries a legacy `.opt span` gold hairline that the Apple override has to reset explicitly.
- **There is no pure white in the palette.** The lightest tokens are `--cream` / `--a-t1` (`#F5F0E8`) and `--white` (`#f7f2ea`), both warm. A `#fff` toggle knob on a warm gold track reads as a foreign object — use `var(--cream)`.
- **Pill buttons** — gold fill for the primary action, neutral outline for the secondary.
- Validation sits **under the field it refers to**, with the hairline above it suppressed so field and message read as one cell.
- `book.html` drives steps from a **frosted footer**; its button proxies each step's hidden `#btn-stepN`, so the original validation still governs it.

### Privacy: the client's address
The address collected at step 3 is deliberately **kept out of the URL**. It travels to Stripe as `metadata[customerAddress]`, and reaches `booking-confirmed.html` via `sessionStorage` (`haloe_address`). Never add it to `success_url` — home addresses in URLs leak into browser history, referrer headers and server logs.

## Compliance (client-facing copy)

- **All client-facing copy must use wellness/symptom language only.** Never claim — explicitly or by implication — to **treat, cure, manage, or heal a named medical condition** (e.g. arthritis, migraine, depression, infertility, IBS, sciatica, etc.). This applies to every customer-visible string: `index.html`, `book.html`, `booking-confirmed.html`, meta descriptions, alt text, and any Stripe/WhatsApp message copy.
- Frame benefits as supporting wellbeing or easing symptoms/sensations, not as medical intervention. Prefer "may help with tension, aches, and relaxation" over "treats back pain"; prefer "supports general wellbeing" over "manages anxiety".
- When editing or adding copy, audit it against this rule before committing. If asked to add a condition-specific claim, push back and reword it into compliant symptom/wellness language.
- Claims removed in July 2026, for reference on what the rule catches: *"draws out stagnant blood, toxins, and impurities to restore the body's natural balance"* (physiological claim), *"one of the oldest and most powerful forms of natural medicine"* (calls it medicine, asserts potency), and a hero line *"addressing pain, hormonal health, fatigue"* (names conditions).
- **Outstanding:** the conditions section is still headed **"Conditions *we treat*"** in `index.html`. The word "treat" above a grid of named conditions is the clearest remaining violation on the site. Flagged with Halima; awaiting her decision on the replacement wording.

## Conventions & gotchas

- Keep CSS/JS inline in each HTML file — that's the established pattern. **One deliberate exception:** `services-data.js` is a shared, external `<script src>` file holding `SERVICES`/`PROMO_DISCOUNT`/`netPrice()`, loaded by both `book.html` and `hijama-manchester.html` so their price lists can't silently diverge. `index.html`'s widget keeps its own separate `HB_SERVICES`/`HB_PROMO_DISCOUNT` (predates this file). Don't "helpfully" re-inline `SERVICES` back into `book.html` — that reintroduces the exact drift this file exists to prevent.
- **Hover styles must be guarded for touch.** On a phone `:hover` sticks to the last-tapped element. A `:hover` rule that out-specifies `.selected` (e.g. `.time-slot:hover` set gold text, `.time-slot.selected` set black) leaves the selection invisible after a tap — the picked slot rendered gold-on-gold. Wrap interactive hover states in `@media (hover: hover)` **and** add `:not(.selected)`. This bit `.time-slot` and `.cal-day` in `book.html`.
- **Autofill fields need an override or they show pale yellow.** Safari/Chrome ignore `background-color` and `color` on an autofilled field. Cover it with an inset `box-shadow` matching the field's surface plus `-webkit-text-fill-color`, and a `transition: background-color 100000s` so the yellow doesn't flash back. Present in all three forms; the inset colour differs per form (see each `:-webkit-autofill` block). On `book.html` the real step-3 fields are `.ios-row` (transparent inputs on a `#1C1C1E` card), **not** the legacy `.form-field` — match the card colour. An `.invalid` row signals with red text, so it keeps `-webkit-text-fill-color: #E5736A`.
- Design tokens are CSS custom properties redefined per file (`--gold #C8A96E`, `--black #0D0D0D`, `--cream #F5F0E8`). Reuse them rather than hard-coding colours. Font is Poppins via Google Fonts.
- **The real WhatsApp number is `447474833643`** — used everywhere. Never reintroduce the old `447700000000` placeholder.
- **The Instagram handle is `@haloe.health`.** The old `@haloe_hijama` survived in six places until July 2026 (the intake guide email, `before-your-session.html`, the homepage footer, the intake error message). Don't reintroduce it.
- **`book.html`'s pay-note promising a WhatsApp confirmation is correct copy, not a bug.** Twilio is inert, so it looks like a broken promise and is tempting to rewrite as email — but Halima messages every client on WhatsApp **herself**. Leave it.
- **The intake form is deliberately not linked or prompted anywhere.** Halima speaks to each client on WhatsApp first and sends `haloe.health/intake` herself. The automatic prompts were removed from all four places that had them — the customer email in `stripe-webhook.js`, `booking-confirmed.html`, `book.html` and `index.html`. The form is still live and still writes to D1; only the prompts are gone. **Do not "helpfully" re-add a link.** `stripe-webhook.js` carries a comment showing how to restore the email block when she wants it back.
- If you change a price or treatment name, update `services-data.js` (drives both `book.html` and `hijama-manchester.html` automatically) **and `HB_SERVICES` in `index.html`** separately — the homepage widget's copy predates `services-data.js` and has silently diverged before (it once advertised facials and peels that were never offered).
- **A 20% promotional discount is active.** `SERVICES`/`HB_SERVICES` keep the **full** price so it can be shown struck through; the amount displayed and charged comes from `netPrice()` (`services-data.js`, shared by `book.html`/`hijama-manchester.html`) / `hbNet()` (`index.html`'s widget). **Never read `svc.price` for money** — it is the pre-discount figure. To end the promotion set `PROMO_DISCOUNT` (in `services-data.js`) and `HB_PROMO_DISCOUNT` to `0`; the strikethroughs vanish and full prices are charged again, with no other edit needed. Keep the two constants in step. The function and confirmation page just echo whatever the wizard sends.
- Mobile calendar disables past dates and Sundays; clinic calendar disables everything except `CLINIC_WEEKDAY` on/after `CLINIC_START_DATE` (see Service model). Mobile time slots are a fixed hard-coded list, 10:00–20:00 in 30-minute steps, duplicated in both `book.html` and `index.html`; clinic time slots come from `CLINIC_SLOT_TEMPLATE`, duplicated the same way.
- **`book.html` slots now reflect real availability** — on date select it fetches `/availability`, then greys out any slot that would overlap an existing booking given the selected treatment's duration (parsed from its `time`). Changing treatment re-validates the chosen slot on return to step 2. It also disables any time **earlier than now when the chosen day is today** (`slotIsPast`), so this-morning's slots can't be booked. The homepage widget does **not** do this (see reservation section above).
- `/book.html?test=1` injects a hidden **£1 test treatment** for live Stripe checks (`SERVICES.massage.unshift`, near the catalogue). It is invisible without the query flag. Remove the block when testing is done.
- The site serves extensionless URLs — `/book.html` redirects to `/book`. Query strings survive the redirect, but `curl` needs `-L` or you'll read an empty 308 and wrongly conclude a deploy failed.
